---
description: Record the remittance - paid in full, short paid, or rejected with the funder's reason word for word.
---

1. Paid: `node scripts/practice.mjs claim paid CLM-... [--amount=] --json`. It clears the invoice behind it. A short payment is named; find out why before it is forgotten.
2. Rejected: `claim reject CLM-... --reason="the funder's words"`. Never paraphrase the reason.
